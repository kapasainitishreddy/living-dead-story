# Chapter Five · The Quiet Orchard

## Playable scope

Continue explicitly from the completed Night Kitchen to a blue-evening orchard station. Choose a handcart ride, walk, or station bench. Every route reaches the same permanent Chapter Five completion with identical prior rewards and NPC decisions. The handcart physically travels between trees; a stationary bench begins seated, supports E/A advancement without standing, and reloads to the same seated rest view; the walking route permits free movement. Optional stable views show the orchard and a pear crate.

Three environmental moments advance only on the player's explicit interaction: a pear falls from a supported branch, two workers carry empty crates through the distant packing-shed doorway, and train light passes across tree trunks. There is no wait requirement, score, timer, breath exercise, meditation instruction, or real-world homework. Short physical presentations can be left with Back; reduced motion settles them instantly. Time outside the game never decays progress.

The saved fictional camera choice, earlier fair, paper sign, dinner choices and Mira's autonomous Lower Mill decision remain intact. Route and each accepted moment save before presentation. Save schema remains 2 with neutral orchard fields for existing snapshots. Malformed orchard traces and cross-chapter chronology are rejected. Save and leave, pause Quit and the OS close request stay open when an orchard save fails, preserving in-memory progress and showing a retry warning.

## Controls

- WASD/arrows or controller left stick move; mouse/right stick orbit
- E/A interacts with the single nearest labelled target
- Enter/A confirms a focused route or continuation
- Esc/B leaves a view or returns from a choice; P/Menu pauses
- R/right-stick click resets the camera
- Pause settings preserve text scale up to 2×, subtitles, reduced motion and independent sound levels
