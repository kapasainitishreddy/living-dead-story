# A Sky That Fits Through the Door · Chapter Three

This local Godot 4.6.3 milestone implements Chapters One–Three. Chapters Four–Seven remain planned and unavailable. It is ready for milestone review, not a store-ready or complete campaign release. Art, including original Noor/Rafi procedural rigs, remains intermediate.

## Playable scope
- Explicit optional conservatory continuation after the preserved Fair completion checkpoint
- Rain-marked glass, warm reading lamps, paper offcut stands, worktable, three rails, measured doorway and stable shadow display
- Noor/Rafi’s gift, knot and sandwich exchange; Mira’s own Lower Mill plan remains independent
- Physical BOATS WITH OPINIONS sign with fixed bent-propeller ornament, leaf and timetable boat
- Crooked/clean/empty star, all six left/center/right permutations, three string lengths and three spare-fold treatments
- Assemble, direct Noor and watch routes; spectator can name a shape to keep or choose their preview
- Every mode completes equally. No balance failure, rating, timer, camera permission or uploaded artwork
- Exact saved geometry is rebuilt in the room and mounted over the original fair booth on revisit
- Optional stable close views, readable low-graphics silhouette, secondary paper/string motion and reduced-motion freeze

## Limits
Cloud graphics use Mesa llvmpipe software OpenGL. This is graphical correctness evidence, not hardware-GPU or mobile performance acceptance. Dummy audio is used because the cloud desktop has no audio output; settings, streams and mixer retirement are tested but human listening is unverified. Physical controllers, Windows/Web runtime, macOS, Android/iOS, signed builds and store release are unverified. Windows/Linux/Web unsigned exports build successfully; only Linux runtime has been verified. No duration claim is made.
