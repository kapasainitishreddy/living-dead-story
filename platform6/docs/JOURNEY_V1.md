# PLATFORM 6 CONTINUES · Journey Mode V1

This is a bounded, authored continuation for review. The main seven-chapter story is finite. Both TAKE THE TRAIN and GO BACK TO SLEEP unlock the same Journey entry after credits. A new completed campaign does not enter without an explicit choice. Later launches resume only a saved active Journey. The campaign model and its save format remain unchanged.

## Implemented scope

Ten small, separate destinations: Rain Town, Unsent Letters Library, Arcade, Beach Before Sunrise, Winter Orchard, Rest Room, Failed Things Festival, Sunrise Cafe, No Destination Road and Fireflies. Each has three authored beats, three local choices, first/return text, independent recurring characters and remembered location states. These are modest original-geometry scenes, not a seamless large world or final production art.

The train is a loading hub. Its board keeps visit history, shelves remember ordinary destination objects and prior campaign continuity remains visible. Compatible time/weather states recombine deterministically from saved visits. Changed props and authored local-choice callbacks support returns. Ten destinations and 30 choices do not mean unlimited new story. No runtime LLM, account, cloud sync, analytics or network service is used.

Five distinct interactive activities:
- Three physical rain-channel switches with a persistent arrangement
- Adjacent envelope/stamp/letter swaps among six arrangements
- A paddle/ball cabinet with actual travel, rebound, recoverable miss and watch path
- Three-point constellation tracing with last-point undo
- Three-component tea blending with a persistent pour

Observation is a sixth, quiet advance-at-your-own-pace activity. No activity gates story access or awards a score. Reduced motion settles functional arcade travel through an equivalent demonstration; decorative weather/motion is quieted. Functional choices remain reversible. Story responses and local state survive returning to the train.

## Optional private writing

Tiny Tomorrows hold the player's exact own words and a chosen SOMEDAY, HAPPENED or NOT ANYMORE state. Future Echoes hold exact words for a validated local YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM. They appear on the private writing shelf when the player returns after that local time. There is no background notification, elapsed-absence penalty, streak or wellbeing reward. Future dates remain sealed on this shelf. Records are capped at 32 of each kind and 1,000 characters each. State changes never rewrite the text. Local timezone/clock changes naturally affect local-wall-clock availability; there is no UTC conversion or scheduled delivery promise.

Private writings are excluded from NPC dialogue, callback generation and campaign data. Save files on the player's own computer are ordinary local JSON, not encrypted storage. No clinical interpretation, crisis inference, prompt to disclose health information or verification of a real-world action is present.

## Saves and comfortable stops

Journey state uses a separate local journey_v1.json and recoverable backups. The store refuses the campaign's canonical save path, future schemas and oversized uninspectable files. Malformed data is preserved, not silently deleted. Each destination retains interrupted authored progress. Return to train, Back, home, pause and quit are available without finishing an activity or scene.

A failed write stays visibly unsaved. Normal quit retries and does not silently abandon progress; an explicit Quit without saving latest Journey changes choice remains available. Returning home requires a successful write. OS/process termination cannot guarantee saving unpersisted memory.
