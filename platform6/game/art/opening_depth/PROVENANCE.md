# Original opening scenic depth

The twilight panorama is original editable SVG authored for STILL HERE with `tools/author_opening_depth.py`, using a deterministic seed. Cloud shapes, wash bands and sparse stars are original geometric brush marks. No third-party art, downloaded media, paid services or image-model outputs are used.

`world/opening_depth.gd` authors the original rooftop facade/roof/window batches, slate deck, rails, entry facade, quiet lamp pools, coastal mountain silhouettes, sea extension and moon-reflection brush strips. Painted masonry reuses this project's original apartment plaster SVG. All decorative geometry is static, collision-free, unshadowed and opaque; original interactive landmarks and water animation remain intact.
