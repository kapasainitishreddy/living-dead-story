# Original Platform 6 station art

All atlas illustrations, shader code, facade compositions and lettering in this directory were authored specifically for Platform 6, October 3, 2026. No external texture, character, music or Sakura Crossing code/assets are copied. The atlas is an original deterministic SVG source imported directly by Godot (1024 × 512 pixels). Reference influence is limited to broad painterly material principles: cool shadow hue, warm highlights and selective visual detail.

Cells: stone wear, wood grain, plaster repair, carriage enamel, canopy painted panels, distant opaque facade, illustrated sea poster, illustrated mountain poster. Posters use original geometric landscapes and no borrowed logos. Station typography uses the existing engine font. Procedural characters remain placeholders.
